04 / Field notes

Learn the why.

Short, practical explainers that help you make better choices before spending your budget.

BUYING GUIDE / 04 MIN01 ↗

CPU vs GPU: where should the money go?

For gaming, resolution and target frame rate strongly influence GPU needs. Competitive high-FPS games can also benefit from a fast CPU. Match the parts to the games you play.

FOUNDATIONS / 03 MIN02 ↗

RAM, storage and the feel of a fast PC.

Memory capacity helps with multitasking; SSDs improve load times and responsiveness. Neither replaces the graphics card for demanding 3D gaming.

SAFETY CHECK / 05 MIN03 ↗

Power supplies: don't cheap out.

Look beyond the wattage number: quality, protections, connectors, efficiency, transient handling and warranty matter for reliability.

THERMALS / 04 MIN04 ↗

Airflow is a component too.

Plan intake and exhaust, leave room for the GPU, manage cables and check cooler height and radiator support. A beautiful case still needs a path for air.

COMPATIBILITY / 06 MIN05 ↗

The five checks before checkout.

Confirm CPU socket and BIOS, memory generation, GPU length, PSU connectors and case/cooler clearance. Check the exact revision, not just the product family.

VALUE / 03 MIN06 ↗

Build for your real workload.

Write down the games and apps you use, target resolution, monitor refresh rate and budget. Spend where it changes your day; keep headroom for future upgrades.
